Dao T, Gu A. Transformers are SSMs: Generalized Models and Efficient Algorithms Through Structured State Space Duality.

Paper

Mamba-2 is not just “Mamba but faster.” Its contribution is an abstraction: structured state space duality (SSD) connects state-space recurrence and attention-like matrix computation. This makes it possible to reason about SSMs with tools familiar from efficient attention.

SSD Roadmap

The figure summarizes the relationship among state space models, semiseparable matrices, structured masked attention, and efficient algorithms. The key point is that recurrence and attention-like computation can be two views of structured sequence mixing.

Why This Matters For Inference

Asymptotic complexity and GPU efficiency are not the same thing. A linear-time recurrence may still be slow if it cannot be parallelized well. SSD enables block algorithms that look more like matrix multiplication, making better use of accelerators.

Architecture Changes

Mamba-2 also simplifies and scales the Mamba block. The paper’s practical value comes from both the theoretical duality and the implementation path: better sequence mixing with GPU-friendly computation.

Takeaway

Mamba-2 shows that the “attention vs SSM” boundary is not clean. Both can be viewed as structured sequence transformations. That abstraction is useful for designing long-context architectures and kernels.
