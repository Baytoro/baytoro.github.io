PC-DARTS: Partial Channel Connections for Memory-Efficient Architecture Search

H.J. Chen
  2021-01-23 2021-01-23 Created  
  • Work hard 
  • >
  • PaperReading 
 
  • NAS 

Xu Y, Xie L, Zhang X, et al. PC-DARTS: Partial Channel Connections for Memory-Efficient Architecture Search. ICLR 2020.

Paper

PC-DARTS is an engineering-focused improvement to DARTS. The problem is simple: DARTS keeps all candidate operations active in a mixed operation, which is memory-heavy and can be unstable. PC-DARTS applies the mixed operation to only a subset of channels and bypasses the rest.

Partial Channel Connection

For an edge, DARTS computes a weighted sum of all candidate operations. PC-DARTS sends only part of the feature channels through that mixed operation:

The remaining channels are passed through directly and concatenated back. This reduces memory and computation during search.

The figure shows both partial-channel operation evaluation and edge normalization. The method tries to make DARTS cheaper without fully abandoning its differentiable search setup.

Edge Normalization

Partial-channel sampling introduces bias. Some edges and operations may receive different amounts of training signal. PC-DARTS adds edge normalization so that topology selection is not dominated by artifacts of partial evaluation.

This matters because DARTS-style search already has a known tendency to over-select skip connections or collapse toward shallow paths. Reducing compute should not make the ranking less reliable.

Efficiency And Bias

The key assumption is that a subset of channels is enough to estimate the relative quality of candidate operations. That is often true, but it is still a proxy. Operations that depend strongly on cross-channel interaction can be misranked.

I would check two correlations in reproduction:

  • search-time ranking vs full-channel retraining ranking;
  • inherited validation accuracy vs final trained accuracy.

Results

PC-DARTS reports competitive CIFAR and ImageNet results with much lower search memory cost. It became a practical DARTS-family baseline because it addresses a real implementation bottleneck.

Takeaway

PC-DARTS does not change the conceptual objective of differentiable NAS. It makes the supernet cheaper and more stable by evaluating operations on partial channels and correcting the resulting bias. This is exactly the kind of detail that decides whether a NAS method is usable in practice.
